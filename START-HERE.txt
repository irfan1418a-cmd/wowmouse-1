WOW MOUSE - EASY GITHUB BUILD
About 10 minutes. No Android Studio needed. You only need a browser and a free GitHub account.

1. Go to github.com and sign up (free). Confirm your email.

2. Click the + (top right), then "New repository".
   Name it: wowmouse     Choose: Private     Click "Create repository".

3. Click "uploading an existing file".
   Drag in EVERYTHING that is inside this folder (select all the files and folders
   inside it, not the folder itself). Click "Commit changes".

4. Add the build file by hand (hidden folders often fail to upload).
   If you can already see a folder called ".github" in your repository, skip this step.
   - Click "Add file", then "Create new file".
   - In the name box type exactly:   .github/workflows/build.yml
     (when you type each / the box turns it into a folder)
   - Open the file PASTE-THIS-build.yml.txt, copy ALL of it, paste it in the big box.
   - Click "Commit changes".

5. Click the "Actions" tab.
   If it asks, click "I understand my workflows, go ahead and enable them".
   The build starts by itself and takes about 5 minutes. Wait for a green check.

6. Get the APK (two ways):
   - On the main page of the repository click "Releases" (right side), open the newest
     one, and click wowmouse.apk under "Assets".
   - Or click the finished run, scroll to "Artifacts", download "wowmouse-apk", unzip it.

7. Put wowmouse.apk on the watch (for example Google Drive: upload it on your phone,
   then on the watch tap the 3 dots next to it and choose Download, then open it from Files).
   The watch must be online and must allow "install unknown apps".

8. On the watch: open Wow Mouse, allow Bluetooth, tap "Make discoverable", then pair the
   watch from your computer's Bluetooth settings. Tap "Connect to device" if it does not connect.

IF THE BUILD FAILS (red X):
Click the failed run, click the step with the red X, copy the error text and send it to Claude.
